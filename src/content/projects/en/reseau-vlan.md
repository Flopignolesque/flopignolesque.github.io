## Context

Networks & Telecom university project: design, cable and configure a local network.

## What I did

- Cabled the network.
- Created **VLANs** on the switches to separate the machines.
- Configured the **router** so the VLANs can talk to each other, on **Cisco IOS**.
- Built and tested the setup in **Packet Tracer**.
